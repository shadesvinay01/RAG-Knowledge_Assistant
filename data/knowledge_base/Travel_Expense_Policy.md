# Enterprise Travel & Business Expense Policy

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2025  
**Document ID:** FIN-EXP-2025  
**Department:** Finance / Operations  

## 1. Flight & Lodging Rules
All business flights exceeding 6 hours are eligible for Business Class booking. Domestic flights must be booked in Economy Class via the corporate travel portal at least 14 days in advance.

## 2. Daily Per Diem Allowance
- Domestic per diem allowance for meals and incidentals: **\$75 USD / day**.
- International per diem allowance: **\$120 USD / day**.

## 3. Receipt Submission Threshold
Original itemized receipts are required for all individual expense claims exceeding **\$25 USD**. Submissions must be uploaded into the expense portal within 30 days of travel completion.
