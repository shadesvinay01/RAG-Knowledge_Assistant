# Customer Support & Billing: Enterprise Tier Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Document ID:** FIN-REF-ENT-2025  
**Department:** Finance / Billing  
**Plan Tier:** Enterprise  

## 1. Enterprise Subscription Refund Eligibility
Enterprise customers are eligible for a **100% full refund** within **60 days** of subscription purchase or renewal if the platform SLA (99.99% uptime) is breached or if key committed features fail to perform as documented.

## 2. Cancellation & Notice Period
Enterprise agreements can be cancelled with a **30-day written notice**. Unused prepaid quarterly or annual subscription balances will be refunded pro-rata without early termination fees.

## 3. Custom Integration & Professional Services Refunds
Professional services fees and custom integration consulting charges are refundable up to 50% prior to milestone sign-off. Once milestone sign-off is completed, service fees are non-refundable.

## 4. Refund Processing Timeframe
Enterprise refund requests are processed within **3 business days** directly via wire transfer or dedicated billing ledger adjustment.
