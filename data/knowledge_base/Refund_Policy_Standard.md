# Customer Support & Billing: Standard Tier Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Document ID:** FIN-REF-STD-2025  
**Department:** Finance / Billing  
**Plan Tier:** Standard  

## 1. Standard Subscription Refund Eligibility
Standard tier customers are eligible for a **full refund** within **14 days** of initial subscription activation. After 14 days, subscriptions are non-refundable.

## 2. Cancellation & Monthly Billing
Standard subscriptions are billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time before the next billing cycle. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing month. No partial monthly refunds are granted.

## 3. Add-on Features & Storage Overages
Add-on seat purchases and extra storage credits are strictly non-refundable once activated.

## 4. Refund Processing Timeframe
Approved Standard tier refunds are processed within **10 business days** back to the original credit card payment method.
