# Enterprise HR Leave Policy (Version 2024.1)

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Expiry Date:** December 31, 2025  
**Document ID:** HR-POL-2024-001  
**Department:** Human Resources  
**Classification:** Internal / All Employees  

## 1. Annual Paid Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to **20 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at a rate of 1.66 days per completed month of service.

## 2. Sick Leave & Medical Absences
Employees receive **10 days** of paid sick leave annually. A certified doctor's certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive business days.

## 3. Carry-Forward Rules
Up to **5 days** of unused annual leave may be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year. Carried-over leave must be utilized before March 31, after which it will expire automatically without compensation.

## 4. Parental & Maternity Leave
Primary caregivers are eligible for 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 2 weeks of paid leave.

## 5. Emergency Leave Limit
Employees may take up to 3 days of emergency leave annually, deducted from their annual leave balance.
