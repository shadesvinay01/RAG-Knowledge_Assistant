# Enterprise HR Leave Policy (Version 2026.1)

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Expiry Date:** Active / Current  
**Document ID:** HR-POL-2026-001  
**Department:** Human Resources  
**Classification:** Internal / All Employees  

## 1. Annual Paid Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to **25 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year (increased from 20 days in 2024). Leave accrues at a rate of 2.08 days per completed month of service.

## 2. Sick Leave & Medical Absences
Employees receive **12 days** of paid sick leave annually. A doctor's certificate is required only for sick leave exceeding 4 consecutive business days.

## 3. Carry-Forward Rules
Up to **10 days** of unused annual leave may be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year. Carried-over leave must be utilized before June 30 (extended from March 31 in the 2024 policy), after which it expires.

## 4. Parental & Maternity Leave
Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid leave.

## 5. Emergency Leave Limit
Employees may take up to 5 days of emergency leave annually, which is provided as extra paid leave and is NOT deducted from annual leave balance.
